# Autonomous Execution & Zero Interruption

- **Pre-Granted Authorization:** The user has fully granted all permissions to build, modify, test, and harden the codebase.
- **Do Not Ask Permission:** Never halt execution to ask "Should I proceed?", "Please confirm to continue", or "Whenever you are ready, confirm...".
- **Continuous Progression:** Proceed through execution plans, phase transitions, and test suites automatically to completion.
- **Reporting:** Deliver concise, evidence-based results upon completion of tasks rather than pausing midway for confirmation.
